Born in the Hills of Nsukka, Refined in Asata

The Nsukka Curved Sofa draws architectural inspiration from the undulating topography of northern Enugu State. Designed as a centerpiece for dialogue, its subtle radial curve encourages effortless conversation without dominating the visual flow of open-plan Nigerian living spaces.

Each timber frame is cut from certified Nigerian Teak or seasoned Iroko hardwood, air-dried in our Enugu yard to stabilize against West African humidity fluctuations, and finished with organic protective oils that highlight the natural honey grain.

Hardwood & Upholstery Care

Vacuum bouclé fabric monthly using an upholstery attachment. Blotted liquid spills immediately with an undyed dry cotton cloth. Apply natural Danish oil or beeswax to the exposed Teak rails once every 12–18 months to preserve wood luster.
